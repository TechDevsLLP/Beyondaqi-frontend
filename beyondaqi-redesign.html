Frequently Queried Data
What is causing high pollution in Mumbai right now?+
Mumbai's current elevated AQI is primarily driven by vehicular emissions, construction dust, and industrial activity near the harbour. Sea breeze patterns from the SE are temporarily trapping pollutants near ground level.
Which areas of Mumbai have the cleanest air?+
Air quality varies across Mumbai based on proximity to traffic corridors, industrial zones, and green spaces. Areas with more vegetation, away from main roads and industries, typically have 10–30% better AQI.
Is it safe to go for morning walks/runs in Mumbai?+
With an AQI of 160 (Poor), moderate outdoor exercise is possible but sensitive groups should avoid it. The best window is before 7am when traffic is minimal. Wear an N95 mask during exercise lasting over 20 minutes.
How bad is Mumbai's air pollution compared to other major cities?+
Mumbai ranks #2 among metro cities today with an AQI of 160. Delhi is currently worse at 162. Mumbai's coastal location generally provides some relief vs inland cities, but marine layer inversion can occasionally trap pollution.
What's the best time to commute in Mumbai to avoid pollution exposure?+
Early morning (5–7am) generally has the lowest AQI. Rush hours (8–10am and 6–9pm) see the highest pollution due to traffic. Late night after 11pm is also relatively cleaner.
